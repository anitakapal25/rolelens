Senior Backend Engineer, Data Platform
Meridian Health

About the role
Meridian Health builds data infrastructure for hospital networks. You will design the services that
move clinical and claims data between providers and payers.

Responsibilities
Design and operate Python services that ingest clinical and claims data at scale.
Build streaming pipelines on Apache Kafka and batch workflows on Airflow.
Own PostgreSQL schemas and query performance for multi-terabyte datasets.
Work with compliance to keep every pipeline HIPAA compliant.

Requirements
6+ years of professional backend engineering experience.
Strong Python and SQL.
Production experience with Apache Kafka or a similar event-streaming platform.
Experience running services on AWS.
Understanding of healthcare data privacy rules such as HIPAA.

Nice to have
Experience with the FHIR or HL7 healthcare data standards.
dbt for analytics engineering.
